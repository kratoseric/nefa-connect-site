Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Nefa Connect ("we", "our", "us") operates the Nefa Connect mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our app. By using Nefa Connect, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Data: Email, username, display name, profile photo, bio, location (if provided)
  • Authentication: Supabase authentication identifiers and Google/Apple OAuth tokens
  • Profile: Hobbies, profession, interests for matchmaking

Usage & Content Data

  • Posts & Interactions: Content you create, spread, resist, comments, likes
  • Social Graph and Messages: Friend requests, connections, private message text, media, voice notes, files, read receipts, and block/report information. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Location: Precise device location may be accessed while using nearby, map, posting, or business features. Social profile/post coordinates are stored in a coarsened form; business locations may remain precise.
  • Business Data: Business listings, catalogues, reviews, enquiries, promotions, and owner-submitted verification documents.
  • Purchases: Subscription product IDs, store receipts or purchase tokens, entitlement status, renewal state, and expiry dates.
  • Referrals: Random expiring referral tokens, share surface, platform, attribution method, and aggregate activation state. Referral links do not contain your email, username, profile ID, location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

Technical Data

  • Device Info: Device model, OS version, app version, unique device identifiers
  • Push Notifications: FCM/APNs tokens for notifications
  • Advertising: Consent choices, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad impressions, and interactions processed by Google AdMob

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve the app's core features (feed, map, chat, matchmaking)
  • Authenticate and secure your account
  • Personalize your experience (matchmaking, content ranking)
  • Show the epidemic map and viral spread visualization
  • Send push notifications (messages, friend requests, viral alerts)
  • Display personalized ads via Google AdMob (see section 6)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in these cases:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (authentication, database, edge functions and storage), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push delivery), Apple and Google (sign-in and in-app purchases), and Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Public Content: Posts you spread, your public profile, and username are visible to other users
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights/safety
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with notice)

5. Your Rights & Controls

  • Access & Edit: Edit your profile, bio, hobbies, and location in-app
  • Delete Account: Use the "Delete Account" option in Settings — this permanently removes your data within 30 days
  • Location: Disable location access in device settings
  • Notifications: Manage push notifications in device settings
  • Data Export: Contact support to request a copy of your data

6. Advertising & Analytics

Nefa Connect displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, consent choices and ad interactions. Where required, tracking or personalized advertising is used only after applicable permission or consent. NefaConnect Plus removes in-app advertising while the entitlement is active.

On Android, the official Google Play Install Referrer API may recover a first-party referral token after installation. On iOS, referral attribution requires reopening or manually submitting the original invite. We do not use device fingerprinting or automatically read your clipboard.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Posts & interactions: Retained unless you delete them or your account
  • Chat messages: Retained until deleted by you or the other party
  • Subscription and transaction records: Retained as required for entitlement verification, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations
  • Deleted account data: Purged within 30 days of deletion request

8. Children's Privacy

Nefa Connect is not intended for children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us to have it deleted.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, which may differ from your country. Applicable provider safeguards and legal transfer mechanisms govern those transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
